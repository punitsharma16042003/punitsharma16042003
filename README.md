### 🌟 About Me

I am a **Research Chemist, Applied Nanochemist, and Scientific Developer** with deep expertise in analytical characterization, environmental monitoring, and nanomaterials synthesis. Currently working as a **Project Associate at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), Ropar**, I combine rigorous wet-lab experimentation with modern computational methods and **AI-assisted vibe coding** to engineer intelligent scientific applications.

- 🔬 **Research Frontier**: Green synthesis of Carbon Quantum Dots (CQDs), environmental sensing, heavy metal remediation, and comprehensive water & air pollution analytics.
- 🏛️ **Institutional Work**: Conducting structural/technical audits for distilleries & ethanol plants with the Punjab Excise Dept., and river water quality monitoring with the Punjab Pollution Control Board (PPCB).
- 💻 **Computational & Vibe Coding**: Passionate about rapid scientific prototyping, building laboratory automation engines, LIMS, and chemical data tools using Python, JavaScript, and AI tools.
- 🎓 **Education**: Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Chemistry (2024–2026) | Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) (2021–2024).
- 🧭 **Core Philosophy**: Leveraging interdisciplinary science, deep research, and automated intelligence to solve real-world industrial and environmental challenges.
